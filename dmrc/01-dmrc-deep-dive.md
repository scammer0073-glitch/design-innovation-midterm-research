# DMRC Delhi Metro - Base to Indepth

## 1. Base / Origin
- Registered 03-05-1995, 50:50 JV Govt of India + GNCTD. HQ Metro Bhawan.
- Why: RITES 1984 report + Delhi traffic crisis. MD E.Sreedharan 1997-2011, rule: delay cost Rs 1.5cr/day Ph-I, Rs 2.5cr/day Ph-II.
- JICA ODA loan 30yr + 10yr moratorium 1.2-2.3%: Ph-I Rs 6434cr of Rs 10571cr (~60%), Ph-II Rs 9686cr (~49%), Ph-IV II 102932M Yen Mar-2024, Addl 79726M Yen Mar-2025.
- First run: Shahdara-Tis Hazari 8.4km 6 stn 25-Dec-2002.

Phases:
- I 1998-2005: 64.75km/59, Rs 10571cr, 2yr9mo early. Red/Yellow/Blue, Broad gauge 1676mm, 3 depots.
- II 2006-2010 (CWG): 123.3km/86, Rs 23810cr. Green/Violet/Airport, shift to Standard gauge 1435mm.
- III 2011-2021: 160.07km/109, Rs 35242cr. Pink 71.55km loop, Magenta, Grey. CBTC + UTO ready.
- IV 2019-2026: Priority 65.1km/45, ~Rs 45000cr full. 2 sections opened 08-Mar-2026. Full 103km/79 + Rithala-Kundli 26.46km.
- Today: 394.25km/289 stn/12 lines per AR 2024-25, ~416.5km/303 stn with NCR 2026. 18 depots + 2 yards, 29-31 interchanges. Aqua + Rapid Metro operated by DMRC.

## 2. How It Works - Technical
- Gauge: Ph-I Broad 1676mm (Red/Yellow/Blue). Ph-II+ Standard 1435mm, coach 3.2m wide, 4/6/8-car.
- Power: 25kV AC OHE (flex elevated, rigid ROCS UG), 33kV aux + SCADA. Regen braking ~35%, 5.26 kWh/km back, ~110MU/yr Blue Line. CDM UNFCCC 1351, Gold Standard GS1246, Solar 42.4MWp rooftop + 345MU/yr Rewa = ~60% solar.
- Signalling: CBTC Pink/Magenta (moving block, ATP/ATO/ATS). OCC Shastri Park + backup. UTO driverless ~29%, 80 trains, Pink+Magenta. Headway 7min (2003) > 2min18sec now.
- AFC DS24 Hitachi: 7000+ devices 269+44 stn, cloud back-office. Evolution: 2002 Token > 2010 Card > 2018 QR Airport > 2020 NCMC Airport > 2023 NCMC+QR full > 2024 WhatsApp/PhonePe/Amazon Pay/ONDC > Virtual Smart Card Sarthi. 2026 split: Paper QR 18%, Mobile QR 9%, NCMC 20%, VS 3%. QR validity 65min diff stn, 30min same. Max 6 pax. WhatsApp 9650855800.

## 3. Service Journey (8 steps for blueprint)
1 Entry numbered gates 1-8 + trilingual totem. 2 CISF XBIS security, baggage 25kg 80x50x30cm, helpline 155655/155370. 3 Tap NCMC/card or scan QR, swing barriers PRM. 4 Platform LED/audio, women coach, tactile edge, PSD new stns. 5 Ride 05:00-23:30. 6 Interchange colour + platform nos (Kashmere Gate triple). 7 Exit tap. 8 Last-mile feeder e-bus/e-auto, DTC, Rapido/Uber via ONDC, One Delhi + Sarthi App (locker/courier).
- Ops: 343 trains/2368 coaches/4508 trips/day/140112 train-km/day. Punctuality 99.95% 2026 (delay >59sec), MDBF 2.72M car-km, COMET Top-5.

## 4. Ecosystem Map (5 layers)
A State+Finance: GoI/GNCTD equity, JICA debt, DDA land, MoHUA NCMC policy.
B DMRC Operator+Consultant: Delhi NCR + Aqua for NMRC + Mumbai Line-3 33.5km + Chennai Ph-II 118.1km + Kochi/Jaipur/Lucknow/Dhaka. Subsidiary Delhi Metro International for last-mile.
C Tech+Payments: Hitachi AFC, Alstom/BEML/Bombardier stock (312 cars Alstom 312M euro Ph-IV), Airtel RuPay NCMC, Paytm/PhonePe/WhatsApp/ONDC.
D Last-mile: DTC/DIMTS (DTC on ONDC Sep-2024), feeders, Rapido/Uber/Namma Yatri, e-rick 35.9%, walk 38.7%, feeder bus only 2.5%.
E Users: 64.6L/day avg 2025 (235.8cr/yr), 62.39L/day 2024, 82k/day 2003. Peak 81.87L 08-08-2025. 55% of India 1.15cr daily metro trips.
Revenue: farebox (hike Rs1-4 Aug-2025) + non-fare property/retail/co-brand/ads/parking + consultancy/O&M/carbon credits.

## 5. Design Innovations
- Wayfinding: 10 colours + discs, trilingual, numbered gates, interchange walk-time, in-train LED strips.
- Accessibility: tactile strips, lifts all new stns, swing barriers, reserved seats, nursing rooms, Braille.
- Station typology: elevated box, UG island/side (Rajiv Chowk, Chawri 2nd deepest, Kashmere triple), green + solar, art (INA craft, Chandni vault, Airport sleek).
- Phygital: token hygiene > contactless > paper QR unbanked > mobile QR > NCMC MaaS. Sarthi super-app.
